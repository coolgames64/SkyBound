## Ram-air turbine references

The simulator's deployable ram-air turbine (RAT) geometry is newly authored in C# from primitives; no third-party RAT model or manual artwork was downloaded or incorporated. The game treats the RAT, including the DC-10/MD-11 air-driven generator (ADG), as a simplified emergency power source. Real aircraft differ in whether the turbine supplies hydraulic power, electrical power or both, its installation and deployment logic. The shared game component does not reproduce those aircraft-specific systems.

RAT availability is enabled for A320, MD11, B757, B767, B777, B787, A220, A300, A310, A330, A340, A350, A380, CONCORDE, DC10 and E190. The following references support the family choices; exact installation geometry remains an approximation.

## Cockpits, cabins and engine animation

All 23 selectable aircraft and saved aircraft designs use the imported FlightGear A320 flight-deck geometry, credited to Thorsten Herrmann and the IDG A320 Family contributors in the A320 source bundle. The cockpit keeps its original scale and pilot-eye relationship; each other airframe positions the same deck near its nose. Live game instruments are shown on the HUD. This is a shared A320 interior, rather than a replica of every manufacturer's flight deck. The newly authored instrumented cockpit remains a fallback only if the included A320 resource is unavailable.

Passenger cabins are newly authored meshes generated when cabin view is first opened. Cabin width, length, seating layout and row count adapt to the selected aircraft family. The v5 interior includes contoured seats, headrests, armrests, trays, pockets and belts, woven fabric/carpet textures, floor rails, curved opaque sidewalls, recessed oval window frames, overhead-bin seams/latches and passenger-service details, galley fittings, bulkheads and EXIT signs. Eight material meshes replace per-seat objects; there are no cabin colliders, real-time lights or per-frame cabin scripts. Three procedural textures belong to each cabin instance and are destroyed with its meshes/materials. Windows have open centres, so the outside world remains visible. These shared cabins do not reproduce each airline's seat map or furnishing. No paid interior models were used; the new geometry and textures are authored in the included C# source.

The A320, DC-10, E190 and ATR72 animate their separated imported fan or propeller meshes. The other aircraft receive newly authored rotating blades and spinners placed using measured engine geometry from their included source models; the generic Regional jet uses approximate rear-engine placement, and Concorde uses approximate compressor axes within its paired intakes. The retained exterior sources are listed above. Generated rotor faces sit slightly forward of stationary fan plates retained in merged exterior meshes. Visual rotation follows the independently spooled left/right engines, includes airflow windmilling and freezes when paused. Its presentation speed is limited to reduce strobing and is not a physical RPM indication.

## Saved aircraft designs

A saved design reuses an included exterior model, with a custom display name, per-instance colour tint and bounded thrust/control settings. It creates no additional detailed aircraft geometry. Tint uses renderer material property blocks; shared source materials, cockpit, cabin and RAT meshes retain their original appearance. The base model's source attribution, dimensions, cabin-family layout and emergency-equipment availability remain attached to that model. Up to 20 aircraft designs are saved as local PlayerPrefs JSON and do not change the third-party model licences above. Selecting a stock airframe restores its original tint and shared default handling.

## Challenges and local creations

The 20 included challenges are newly authored game data: the seven original scenarios plus 13 distinct system-failure or recovery/completion objectives. The repeated numeric practice grid has been removed. Airport-specific missions use exact runway directions from the bundled public-domain data; the drills are fictional training configurations. The custom challenge builder stores up to 100 additional definitions locally as PlayerPrefs JSON, with edit/delete/reload support. Neither the included missions nor custom definitions import third-party accident reconstructions or additional scenery assets.

Menu starts and retries depart from a nearest suitable real paved runway. Emergency challenges are staged: take off with engines and departure fuel available, reach the indicated approach-entry beacon, then arm the configured emergency conditions and timers. All 23 stock airframes and saved designs use the same imported A320 cockpit described above. Passenger cabins, the shared flight model, RAT equipment and exit-slide layouts remain game approximations.

## Real runway data

[OurAirports open-data downloads](https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data) provide the airport and runway records. The maintainer publishes daily CSV downloads and dedicates the data under the repository's [public-domain licence](https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data/blob/main/LICENSE). Primary documentation is at [OurAirports data](https://ourairports.com/data/); the maintainer's direct inputs are [airports.csv](https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/airports.csv) and [runways.csv](https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/runways.csv).

The project bundles the **2026-10-11** snapshot under `DataSources/OurAirports`: original `airports.csv`/`runways.csv`, `SOURCE.json` with their SHA-256 hashes and source URLs, and `convert_runways.py`. The resulting game catalog contains **9,718 directional entries representing 4,859 runways**, with one entry for each end. It includes open airports/runways with a paved-surface code, both endpoint coordinates, length at least **1,800 m** and catalog width at least **20 m**. Missing source width is replaced with 148 ft (approximately 45 m); headings are computed from the published endpoints, and feet are converted to metres. Closed airports/runways, missing endpoints and coordinates outside the supported latitude/longitude range are excluded.

This factual dataset positions runway departures in the streamed world; it does not supply 3D airport models. Catalog suitability is a game filter, and the community snapshot can differ from current airport details or imagery. All nearest-runway starts preserve the existing shared flight model and approximate contact geometry. No native Android/iOS app or physical-phone performance result is included in this release.
