Skybound MCAS is a simplified gameplay approximation, fitted only to 737 MAX profiles. It requires airborne manual flight, flaps fully up, available power and two agreeing simulated angle-of-attack inputs. It applies one bounded nose-down correction per high-AOA event, rearming after sustained low AOA. Pilot pull input overrides commands; the MCAS cutout button or K stops further commands. Gameplay thresholds, timing and trim authority are not certified aircraft values. The flight-assist mode inhibits the simulation. References: https://www.boeing.com/737-max-updates/mcas/ and https://www.boeing.com/commercial/737max/737-max-update/737-max-software-updates .
