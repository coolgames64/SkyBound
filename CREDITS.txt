SKYBOUND FLIGHT SIMULATOR
A recreational flight game with simplified flight dynamics.

AIRBUS A320 EXTERIOR & FLIGHT DECK
FlightGear IDG A320 Family, GPL version 2.
Repository: github.com/legoboyvdlp/A320-family
Revision: fec1ecba767c8fffd145337b27012e89c01e3ade

3D and textures: Semir Gebran (CaptB).
Cockpit: Thorsten Herrmann (TH-555).
Contributors include Josh Davidson (Octal450), Jonathan Redpath (legoboyvdlp), Nia / merspieler, Matthew Maring, Andrea Vezzali and the FlightGear community.

Converted from AC3D to FBX, with Swiss exterior textures, static flight-deck geometry, and simplified opaque exterior glazing. Source geometry, textures, licence and conversion scripts are in ModelSources/A320. FlightGear systems and flight dynamics are not included.

SCENERY NOTES
Internet access is needed. Imagery dates, resolution and colour vary between regions. Buildings and trees are part of the aerial image rather than separate 3D objects. Cockpit switches are static; use the keyboard for game controls.
